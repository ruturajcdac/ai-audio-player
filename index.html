Privacy Policy — Sonrik Audio Player

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

Sonrik Audio Player is a Chrome extension that intercepts text-to-speech (TTS) audio from Gemini and ChatGPT and presents it in a seekable, controllable audio player within the page. This extension was built to address the UX gap where users cannot seek back or forward in AI-spoken responses.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data whatsoever. The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any user data, browsing history, audio content, or any other information.

How the Extension Works

The extension operates entirely within your browser. It intercepts audio data that is already being sent to your browser by Gemini and ChatGPT, plays it through a custom HTML5 audio player, and optionally allows you to download it. All processing happens locally on your device. No data ever leaves your browser through this extension.

Permissions Used

  • storage: Used only to remember your enabled/disabled preference for the extension. Nothing else is stored.
  • Host access to gemini.google.com and chatgpt.com: Required to inject the audio player into these pages. The extension only reads audio data that is already being delivered to your browser by these services.

Third-Party Services

This extension does not communicate with any third-party servers. It does not use analytics, crash reporting, or any external APIs beyond what Gemini and ChatGPT themselves serve to your browser.
